Choose the situation, then play a sound

Start with a familiar can-opening sound, can lid rattle, feeder sound, toy bell or soft kitten call. Play briefly at low volume, then stop and listen. Every recording is free to play.

Follow a calm 30-minute search

Make the home quiet, check one room fully, and expand outdoors if a door, window, balcony or carrier may have opened. Do not rely on a sound response to decide whether a cat is nearby.
